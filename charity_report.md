# Helping Hands Foundation — Annual Report FY2025

## Mission

Provide clean drinking water to rural communities.

## Financial summary (FY2025)

- Total donations received: $1,000,000
- Program spend (wells, filters, maintenance): $620,000 — 62% of donations
- Administrative costs: $280,000 — 28% of donations
- Fundraising costs: $100,000 — 10% of donations

In FY2025, 62% of donations went directly to programs.

## Programs delivered

- 12 new wells built across three districts
- 4,800 household water filters distributed
- 96 maintenance visits to existing wells

## Notes

- Figures are unaudited management accounts for FY2025.
- The foundation's public website states a higher program-spend share than the figure in this report.
